About Me

I lead Multi-Agent Pathfinding (MAPF) for warehouse robotics automation at GreyOrange. As a Senior Engineering Manager with 13+ years of experience, I combine hands-on backend engineering with system architecture and team leadership.

My work focuses on coordinating paths for fleets of robots to reduce congestion, resolve deadlocks faster, and improve overall navigation efficiency. This includes rearchitecting the software that plans and coordinates robot traffic across a shared warehouse.

I also led the decomposition of a large Erlang monolith into independently deployable microservices, a major part of my architecture work at GreyOrange. My broader experience includes Ranger MoveSmart, where I joined as a founding engineer, and building sortation systems for warehouse automation.

Python, Erlang, and Elixir are at the core of my backend work. My experience also spans frontend development, mobile applications, databases, messaging, and cloud deployments. I enjoy working through architecture decisions, staying close to the code, and mentoring engineers as we take systems from an initial idea to production.

I began my career at HashedIn Technologies, working on a pet hotel management system and helping modernize a job portal. That work included backend development, REST APIs, database design, and search. I hold a B.Tech in Information Technology from IIIT Allahabad.

This is where I share my learnings, ideas, and occasional poetry. Let’s connect over robotics, distributed systems, or engineering leadership.
