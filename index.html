02 System architecture

Decomposing
a monolith.

I led the decomposition of a large Erlang monolith into independently deployable microservices.

The central question: where should one service end and another begin? Clear boundaries let components evolve independently, while keeping their interactions explicit.

A different system, a familiar challenge: coordinating parts without coupling everything together.
